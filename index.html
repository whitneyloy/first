About

I lead the McCarton Foundation, a New York nonprofit that serves children with autism and other developmental disabilities, with a focus on families in underserved communities.

I came up through fundraising, so I've worked as a director of development, run marketing and sponsorship, and produced the events that keep organizations funded. That mix of revenue, storytelling and operations is what I bring to the job now.
